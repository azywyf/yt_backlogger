# Behavior preferences

- Before making any code change (Edit/Write) or running any command in this repo, briefly state in plain language exactly what you're about to do and why — which file(s), what the change is, what command you're running and what it's for — *before* doing it. Don't just perform the action silently and let the diff/output speak for itself; narrate the intent first, in your own words.
- This applies to every action, not just risky ones — including small edits, reads, and routine commands. Keep the explanation short and specific (a sentence or two), not a wall of text.
